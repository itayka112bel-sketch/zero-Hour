# Zero Hour — V1.0

**Zero Hour** is a tactical first-person shooter about private military contractors, secret contracts and the one hour when everything goes wrong.

## Story

In 2031, the world's governments no longer fight their own wars. They hire companies.

**Vanguard Dynamics** is the largest private military company on the planet. Its contractors take any job for the right price: guarding oil fields, seizing ports, retrieving "assets". Every contract ends the same way, with a single word on the screen: *Zero Hour*, the moment the operation begins and there is no way back.

When a routine contract in the port city of **Kovarna** turns into a trap, a small team of veteran operators discovers the truth: someone inside Vanguard sold the mission, and the people on the other side of the gunfire are former colleagues. A rival group, **Black Meridian**, is hunting the same secret: a locked vault hidden beneath the harbor, containing the evidence of who really finances the wars.

Two teams. One vault. Nobody is clean, and nobody gets paid unless they walk out alive.

## Gameplay

- Round-based tactical 5v5 combat in the style of classic competitive shooters
- Attack and defend objectives: plant the charge, hold the site, rescue the hostage
- Economy system: earn money each round and buy weapons and gear
- Realistic weapon handling with recoil patterns and penetration
- Maps built around the story: harbor, oil refinery, abandoned embassy, bunker complex
- High-quality PBR textures and realistic materials
